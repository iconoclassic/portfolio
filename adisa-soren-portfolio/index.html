UX Research & AI Deployment Strategy · New York

Shaping consumer and enterprise experiences across healthcare and finance data platforms

Research leader with over a decade of guiding product strategy across enterprise AI, healthcare technology, and financial services. I de-risk product decisions from discovery through launch and align key partners around evidence, not opinion.

At Verily, that work led to the launch of Lightpath, a multi-sided platform that lets clinicians scale personalized care and patients receive timely, tailored care remotely. At BlackRock, it led to the redesign of Aladdin, the cloud-based platform unifying investment management, risk analytics, trading, and operations, improving efficiency across the portfolio management lifecycle. At Google, it led to credibility features that help news consumers tell reliable sources from misinformation.

I give leadership a clearer path to the right product: fast signal on early bets, firm evidence on major commitments.
